\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
This protocol addresses CP violation with the real Data from the LHCb. Furthermore the focus is on the B Mesons, which decay into three kaons, among other things. The decays of the $\text{B}^+$ Meson and the anti-particle $\text{B}^-$ are traced back and counted to analyze the asymmetry of the matter-anitmatter ratio.
